# GLM-5.3-Flash Maya-M on Project Maya: 61-62 tok/s

**Hardware:** [2x A100 SXM4 40GB + RTX 3090, Ryzen 9 7950X, 96 GB DDR5-3600](README.md) ·
**Engine:** [Project Maya](https://github.com/mw00/project-maya) v1.1.17 (`8d9a9b5`) ·
**Measured with:** [`tools/bench`](../../tools/bench/README.md) · 2026-10-20

## Result

| Config | Decode | Decode @ 32K | Prefill @ 32K |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Maya's setup config (262K context, RAM-resident) | 61.1 tok/s | 48.3 tok/s | 1,339 tok/s |
| + `STRATA_GLM_ROUTE_AFFINITY=0.02` | 61.5 tok/s | 50.4 tok/s | 1,332 tok/s |
| + `STRATA_GLM_ROUTE_AFFINITY=0.04` | 62.5 tok/s | 50.6 tok/s | 1,326 tok/s |

The smaller sibling of [Maya-L](2026-10-20-glm-5.3-flash-maya-l-maya.md) (47.7 tok/s at best on this box): 1.3x the
decode. Route affinity, worth +24% on Maya-L, does almost nothing here (below).

## Config

`configs/maya-m.json`, written by Maya's setup, hand changes: `STRATA_GLM_RAM_RESIDENT=1`, `STRATA_GLM_KV_INT8=1`,
`STRATA_GLM_RAM_HEADROOM_GB=10`.

```json
{
 "args": ["--glm-pack", "/data/models/glm-5.3-flash-maya/Maya-M/pack",
          "--max-context", "262144", "--expert-cache", "auto", "--prefill", "auto", "--kv-streaming", "on",
          "--expert-profile", "/data/maya/project-maya/data/expert-profile-glm.bin"],
 "gpu": [0, 1, 2],
 "sampling": {"temperature": 1.0, "top_p": 0.95},
 "env": {"STRATA_GLM_SLOT_KEEP": "1", "STRATA_GLM_RAM_RESIDENT": "1", "STRATA_GLM_KV_INT8": "1",
         "STRATA_GLM_RAM_HEADROOM_GB": "10", "STRATA_GLM_USAGE": "/data/maya/usage/glm-5.3-flash.txt"}
}
```

For the bench `STRATA_GLM_SLOT_KEEP` and `STRATA_GLM_USAGE` were removed and `exe`/`cwd` point at the v1.1.17 build,
as on the Maya-L page. Started the same way (`./maya.sh --config maya-m.json` in the CUDA container).
**Requests:** thinking disabled, no sampling fields (the config's temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95 apply).

## Where it sits

| | CUDA0 (A100) | CUDA1 (A100) | CUDA2 (RTX 3090) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Layers (auto split) | 0-18 | 19-35 | 36-44 + MTP draft layer |
| VRAM expert pool | 34.92 GB (4,303 slots) | 35.41 GB (4,413 slots) | 20.20 GB (2,386 slots) |
| Pinned RAM tier | 8.26 GB | 9.02 GB | 8.59 GB |

All 12,384 experts (108.1 GB) sit in VRAM + RAM; 95-96% of a token's routed experts hit VRAM. Per answer, the CPU
lane computes 8-26 experts per token in 2-7.5 ms of a 15-21 ms token, so it is a small part of the time, not the
bottleneck it is on Maya-L (17 of 26 ms there). Affinity shrinks it further (after the 32K prompt: 13.8 experts /
4.3 ms per token plain, 7.6 / 2.0 ms at 0.04), but the GPUs' own share of the token stays.

## Every variant

| Variant | Changes against the base | Decode | Last 4 | Decode @ 32K | Prefill @ 32K | Load | Min. MemAvailable |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| `glmm_aff04` | `STRATA_GLM_ROUTE_AFFINITY=0.04` | 62.5 | 59.9 | 50.6 | 1,326 | 41 s | 50.9 GiB |

Decode values in tok/s; prefill over ~27,820 prompt tokens. Exact configs: [`configs/`](2026-10-20-glm-5.3-flash-maya-m-maya/configs/),
per-prompt values: [`raw/`](2026-10-20-glm-5.3-flash-maya-m-maya/raw/results.tsv). The quality cost of affinity was
measured on Maya-L ([there](2026-10-20-glm-5.3-flash-maya-l-maya.md#quality-route_affinity-against-the-plain-path)),
not on Maya-M; for Maya-M it buys too little to be worth it.

Earlier, with 64 GB of RAM and v1.1.15, Maya's own `--bench` (greedy, 3x 256 tokens, different prompts) measured
66.3 tok/s on all three cards and 55.5 tok/s on the two A100s alone; not comparable with the numbers above.
